A printer device can only reach an address a device on that LAN advertises.

01Advertise the printer

An always-on device on the printer LAN should advertise that address, preferably as a /32.

02Approve the route

Approve it for this printer's device in the Tailscale admin console.

03Use the LAN address

Add the printer with that LAN address. Print jobs and toner, drum, paper, and jam readings use this printer's device.
